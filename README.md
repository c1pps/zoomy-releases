Zoomy updates itself: when a new version is out, the app shows what is new and updates in one click.

### First launch

The installers are not signed with a paid certificate yet, so your system may warn you the first time:

- **Windows**: if SmartScreen appears, click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
- **macOS**: if the app is reported as damaged or from an unidentified developer, run `xattr -cr /Applications/Zoomy.app` in Terminal, or open it from **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway**.
- **Linux**: make the AppImage executable (`chmod +x Zoomy-linux-x64.AppImage`) and run it.

## What it does

- **Record** a screen or window, microphone, system audio (Windows, Linux) and webcam, at 30 or 60 fps.
- **Auto-zoom** follows your clicks with smooth, editable zooms.
- **Look**: cinematic cursor, wallpapers, gradients, padding, rounded corners, 3D tilt.
- **Edit**: split, trim, reorder and change the speed of clips, 12 transitions, animated text, effects, noise reduction and music that ducks under your voice.
- **Captions** generated on your device.
- **Share**: save a video to your Zoomy account and send a link where anyone can watch it and comment (5 GB free).

## Pricing

Zoomy is free, with exports up to 1080p and a small "Made with Zoomy" badge.
**Zoomy Pro** (39.99 € once, or 9.99 € a month) adds 4K at 60 fps, removes the badge, and unlocks auto-zoom, 3D tilt, auto captions and image backgrounds. See [usezoomy.com](https://usezoomy.com/#pricing).

## Your videos stay yours

Projects and exports stay on your computer. Nothing is uploaded unless you choose to save a video to your Zoomy account. See the [privacy page](https://usezoomy.com/privacy/).
